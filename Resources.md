## List of Resources

### Player
- Credits
Units of value and exchange
(Accumulates)
- Science
Understanding of reality
(Accumulates, carrying capacity is total ScienceLimit)
- ScienceLimit
Institutional capacity to retain existing scientific understanding
(Pop and Hab upkeep only, does not accumulate)
- Freight
Interstellar Logistic Capacity
(Does not accumulate)
- Stability
Mandate to rule
(Does not accumulate)
- Favor
Diplomatic capital with minor powers
(Accumulates)
- Bureaucracy
Institutional sophistication
(Does not accumulate)

### Physical
(All Accumulate)
- Food
Organic sustenance
- Metal
Common minerals
- Rares
Scarce minerals
- Atomics
Nuclear fuel
- Consumer Goods
Personal property
- Luxury Goods
High value property & status symbols
- Machinery
Metal constructs
- Chips
Information processors
- Nanites
Nanomachinery
- Anti-matter
Mirrored matter, on contact converts to pure energy
- Singularities
Micro-blackholes that convert mass to energy

### Colonial
(All Do Not Accumulate)
- Joy
Pop Satisfaction
- Boost
Lifting capacity from surface to orbit.
- Loyalty
Willingness of this colony to submit to your rule

### Local
(All Do Not Accumulate)
- Energy
Ability to do work
- Production
Industrial capacity
- Compute
Information processing power
- Habitat
Pop Residential Space

### Combat
(All Do Not Accumulate)
- HP
Ability to endure damange
- Damage
Destructive capability
- Armor
Damage Deflection
- AP
Armor Penetration
- Range
Standoff Distance
- Impulse
Change in momentum
- HyperImpulse
Hyperlane motion
- Evasion
Ability to dodge
- Targeting
Ability to hit
- Fear
Flight instinct
- Morale
Ability to overcome fear
